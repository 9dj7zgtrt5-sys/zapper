# Zapper 3.7: home screen (design A, chosen from three mockups)

```
 Good evening                         [⌂ YouTube & apps]   9:41 PM
 ┌──────── Live TV (playing) ───────┐ ┌─ Movies ─┐ ┌─ Series ─┐
 │  channel video inside the tile    │ │ 103,312  │ │  8,940   │
 │  ● LIVE  Global Toronto [4K]      │ │  titles  │ │  shows   │
 └───────────────────────────────────┘ └──────────┘ └──────────┘
 Pick up where you left off
 [previous channel] [Show · S2 E3 · 18 min left] [Movie · 1 h left] …
 OK Open   ◀ ▶ ▲ ▼ Move   Back Watch TV   ☰ Help
```
- Back while watching TV opens Home. Back on Home goes back to TV.
- OK on Live TV opens the channel list. Movies and Series open their screens, and Back returns
  to Home.
- "YouTube & apps" opens the TV's home screen (it replaces the old double-Back).
- Pick-up row: the previous 2 channels, then unfinished movies and episodes (one per show).
- The tab-row experiment from 3.5 is removed. Movies and Series still open on Continue watching.
